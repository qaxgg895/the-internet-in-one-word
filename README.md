A program that writes one word describing the whole internet to `/output/entry.txt`: *"Doomscrolling"*. The internet's defining modern behaviour is not searching, chatting, or shopping — it is the endless thumb-flick through bad news at 2 a.m. that nobody planned, nobody enjoys, and everybody does anyway. The feed never ends, the news never improves, and we never stop scrolling — one word and the whole internet, its business model and its users, is in the room.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
